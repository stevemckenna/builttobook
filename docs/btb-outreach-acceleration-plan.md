# Built To Book: Outreach Acceleration Plan

Last updated: 2026-10-01. Status: working plan.

Purpose: help Steve move from early call traction to a faster sales sprint, with a target of 10 clients as quickly as possible. Steve said he may close the first sale by October 2, 2026 and would love to reach 10 by the end of next week.

---

## Recommendation

Keep selling the same core thing, but split outreach into two lanes:

1. **No website:** lead with the free mockup.
2. **Weak website:** lead with a **Job Leak Check**.

For weak websites, do not open with "your site is bad." Most owners already know it is not perfect, but that does not create urgency. Open with a business gap:

> I noticed you already have a site, so this is not a "you need a website" pitch. The bigger issue is whether it is helping you win the next job. I can send over a quick Job Leak Check showing what I would fix first across Google, proof, and follow-up.

The Job Leak Check should be fast, visual and specific. One page or one short Loom is enough.

## The Job Leak Check

Use this when the business has a website, but it is not helping them book better jobs.

Score only five things:

| Check | What to look for | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Google visibility | GBP complete, correct categories, service area, reviews, photos, map rank signs | They need to show up when nearby buyers search |
| Proof | Real project photos, reviews, before and after work, service area proof | Their work has to look as strong online as it does in real life |
| Fast contact | Tap-to-call, tap-to-text, form, calendar or simple request path | Buyers move to the next company when contact is slow |
| Follow-up | Missed-call text-back, quote follow-up, review requests, CRM | This is where Lead System has the strongest reason to exist |
| Best-job fit | The site says what jobs they want, where they work, and who they are best for | A site should filter toward better jobs, not just any inquiry |

Call the result one of these:

- **3 places better jobs are leaking**
- **The next-job check**
- **Website and Google leak check**

Best name: **Job Leak Check**. It is plain, slightly urgent, and points to money without making a fake promise.

## Best Prospects Right Now

Prioritize companies that already believe in marketing but have obvious gaps:

- Website exists, but weak proof, weak CTA, no follow-up signs, or old design
- 4.4+ rating with fewer reviews than nearby competitors
- Good photos on Google or Facebook, but weak website presentation
- Running ads to a weak site or generic landing page
- Strong trade, visible crews or finished work, but poor online trust
- Owner-operated or established local service business, not a brand-new side hustle

Avoid spending too much time on:

- Companies with no demand signal and no visible work
- Very low review ratings unless there is an obvious comeback story
- Companies that look like they only want the cheapest possible site
- Large companies with internal marketing teams unless there is a warm intro

## Outreach Targets For The Sprint

To reach 10 sales quickly, Steve needs conversations, not just a larger list.

Working sprint target:

| Daily action | Target |
|---|---|
| New researched prospects added | 40 to 60 |
| First-touch messages sent | 25 to 40 |
| Follow-ups sent | 20 to 30 |
| Job Leak Checks or mockups prepared | 3 to 5 |
| Calls booked | 2 to 4 |

This is more aggressive than the daily-actions doc. Use it as a short sprint, not the forever cadence. Stop or reduce volume if deliverability, platform warnings, or reply quality drops.

## Message Angles To Test

### No Website

Subject or opener:

> Quick mockup for [Business Name]

Message:

> Hey [Name], I was looking at [trade] companies in [area] and noticed I could not find a real website for [Business Name]. Your Google profile already gives me enough to sketch what a proper homepage could look like. Want me to send over a free mockup?

### Weak Website

Subject or opener:

> Quick Job Leak Check for [Business Name]

Message:

> Hey [Name], I saw you already have a website, so this is not a "you need a website" note. The bigger question is whether it is helping you win the next job. I noticed a couple gaps around Google, proof, and follow-up. Want me to send a quick Job Leak Check with the first 3 fixes I would make?

### Review Gap

Subject or opener:

> Your reviews are close, but there is a gap

Message:

> Hey [Name], your rating is solid. The opportunity I noticed is the review gap against a few nearby competitors. That usually affects who gets the call first. Want me to send over the quick check I would use to tighten Google, reviews, and follow-up?

### Good Work, Weak Proof

Subject or opener:

> Your work looks stronger than the site

Message:

> Hey [Name], your project photos look solid, but the site does not make that work easy to trust quickly. I build websites and follow-up systems for trades businesses. Want me to send over the 3 changes I would make first?

## Tool Options

### Fastest Stack

Use this first if the goal is speed this week:

- **Scrap.io or Outscraper:** pull Google Maps prospects by trade and city, including website, phone, reviews and ratings.
- **Clay:** enrich owner/contact data and run scoring columns.
- **Semrush Local or Local Falcon:** create proof around Google Maps visibility and review gaps.
- **GHL:** track every prospect, follow-up and call.

### Atlas

There are at least two relevant "Atlas" products, so confirm which one Tyler meant.

- **Atlas Prospect:** prospecting orchestration. It finds companies, finds people, verifies emails and phones, and can run custom research columns. Interesting if Steve wants a more advanced, spreadsheet-style prospecting machine.
- **BuildAtlas.ai:** contractor marketing service. It is more competitor or inspiration than prospecting software. The useful idea is their simple audit hook around visibility in Google and AI search.

Recommendation: ask Tyler for the exact URL and what part is working: finding businesses, scoring them, generating audits, getting contacts, or booking calls.

### Semrush

Use Semrush for audit ammunition, not as the main list builder.

Best uses:

- Google Business Profile audit
- Map rank and competitor comparison
- Review management and review generation angle
- Site audit for obvious technical problems
- Local listing consistency checks
- Keyword and competitor gaps for higher-value Local Authority conversations

Semrush Local Base is currently positioned around GBP audit, automated posts, review replies and map rank tracking. Semrush Local Pro adds listing management, review generation and competitor review analytics.

## Scoring Model

Score every prospect from 0 to 10.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Established local service business | 2 |
| Website is missing or visibly weak | 2 |
| Strong Google rating or photos, but underused proof | 2 |
| Review count behind nearby competitors | 1 |
| Clear service area and profitable job type | 1 |
| Owner or decision-maker contact is findable | 1 |
| Follow-up gap is likely visible | 1 |

Prioritize 7+ for personal outreach. Put 4 to 6 into nurture or later follow-up.

## Offer Path

Lead System should stay the main close for weak-site prospects:

- They already have a site, so the upgrade is not "a website."
- The stronger promise is website plus follow-up, built to capture more of the work they already earned.
- The Job Leak Check naturally points to missed calls, slow replies, quote follow-up and review requests.

Local Authority is the upsell when the biggest gap is Google visibility, service pages, reviews and competitor positioning.

Google Ready is the low-friction fallback only when the owner is not ready for a website rebuild or CRM.
