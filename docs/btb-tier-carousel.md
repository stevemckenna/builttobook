# Tier Escalation Carousel: "Same Bench, Three Levels"

Instagram carousel (5 slides) that also runs as a Reel of stills. Shows what each Built To Book ads package actually puts to work, from Starter to Market Dominator, in one fixed scene that fills up as the price goes up.

- **Status:** Draft for Steve's approval. Nothing posts without his OK.
- **Offer source:** the live site at www.built2book.live (packages section) and try.built2book.live/nov-promo, checked 2026-11-03. The old four website packages are superseded and not used here.
- **Brand source:** Built To Book design system in Claude Design (README and tokens) and `assets/Built-to-Book-Visual-Identity-Guide.pdf`.
- **Goal:** education plus a fair flex. A prospect sees what their money buys at each level and feels the gap. One next step: book a 15-minute fit call.

---

## Before you shoot: three things to confirm

1. **Who really works on accounts.** The crew grows 1, 2, 3 across the tiers. Only show people who actually do that work (you, whoever builds the CRM and automations, whoever edits video). If today it is you covering every role, use the "one operator" fallback noted on each slide: you appear once, and the bench gets more stations instead of more people.
2. **November bonus wording.** The homepage says the bonus (1 edited video ad plus 1 hour of shooting, $500 value) comes with Booked Growth and Market Dominator. The nov-promo page says it applies to any package and that Starter is $1,000 for November sign-ups. The slides use regular prices so the post stays true after November. Pick one bonus rule before the caption goes out.
3. **Launch timeline.** No launch turnaround is written down anywhere. The captions use only the documented cadence (tuned weekly, reported monthly; bi-weekly reviews at Market Dominator). Add a launch timeline only if you will stand behind it.

## The fixed scene (every slide)

**Setting: "The Bench."** A working shop office that doubles as the Built To Book workspace. Concrete floor, a long steel-topped workbench running left to right across the lower third, a plywood wall behind it with a pegboard strip, and a roll-up garage door filling the right third of the back wall. Through the door: a plain white work van, no logos or lettering.

**Camera:** locked off, straight on, eye level (about 1.5 m), 28 mm equivalent, the bench edge parallel to the frame. Same lens, height and position on every slide. Nothing moves except what is added.

**Light:** overhead shop LEDs plus daylight from the door, neutral 5000K, same exposure and grade on every slide. No moody darkening, no dark overlays.

**What never changes:** the bench, the wall, the door frame, the van, the floor, the camera, the grade. That is what makes the escalation obvious.

## Art direction

### Format

- Design each slide at **1080 x 1350 (4:5)** for the carousel.
- For the Reel, extend to **1080 x 1920 (9:16)** by adding a solid Jobsite Ink band top and bottom. Put the Built To Book lockup (reversed) in the top band. Leave the bottom band empty because the Reel interface covers it. Keep all text inside the original 4:5 area.

### Slide layout (4:5, same on every slide)

| Zone | Size | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Top band | 0 to 380 px | Jobsite Ink `#151A18` with a subtle blueprint grid (hairline `#212A26`, 40 px pitch). Signal Green bar on the left edge. Eyebrow "LEVEL 1 OF 3" in Arial Bold uppercase, then the headline in Arial Black uppercase, Concrete White, final phrase in Signal Green. 4 px Blueprint Blue rule under the headline. |
| Level meter | inside the top band, right side | Three square blocks. Filled blocks in Concrete White, empty blocks as hairline outlines. Level 1 = one filled, Level 3 = all three. Square corners, no checks (checks are reserved for booked outcomes). |
| Photo | 380 to 1170 px | The scene, full bleed. No overlay, no tint, no logo on the photo. |
| Bottom bar | 1170 to 1350 px | Signal Green `#C7F36B` proof bar with the caption line in Arial Bold, Jobsite Ink, sentence case. |

- Headline size around 64 px, caption line around 28 px. Square corners everywhere; 8 px maximum radius on the CTA button.
- No gradients, no shadows, no emoji, no extra icons. Safety Orange stays out of this set entirely.

### How each slide must differ from the last

The scene stays fixed; only these change, and each one steps up every level:

| Signal | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| People | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Screens | 1 | 3 | 6 (incl. wall TV) |
| Bench filled | about 25% | about 60% | edge to edge plus cart |
| Wall | small calendar, 2 dates | whiteboard plus 4 campaign blocks | full 12-month calendar, ad proofs grid |
| Roll-up door | closed | half open | fully open, van doors open |
| Phones | 1, face down | 1, lit with a lead alert | 2, one labeled "PRIORITY LINE" |
| Level meter | 1 of 3 | 2 of 3 | 3 of 3 |

If someone flicks through fast, the door, the wall and the head count alone should tell them the level.

### Honesty rules for the images

- **Best option: shoot it for real** in your own office with your own gear. It is your operation, so a real photo is honest and easy to stage.
- **If AI-generated:** add "Illustrative scene" in small Arial Bold in the top band, per the AI imagery rule. Never present it as a client's shop or a client's results.
- Screens show generic interfaces only: no Meta or Google logos or UI copies (the site carries a no-affiliation disclaimer), and no readable performance numbers.
- Only show gear you actually use. If your shoots are on a phone with a gimbal, show that instead of a cinema camera.

### Production tip: build down, not up

Create or shoot the **Level 3 scene first**, fully loaded. Then make Level 2 and Level 1 by removing people and gear from that exact frame (in Gemini, use image editing on the Level 3 image rather than generating fresh). This guarantees the bench, angle, light and van match perfectly across all slides.

### Gemini starter prompt (Level 3 base image)

> Photorealistic wide shot, locked-off camera, straight on at eye level, 28mm lens. A clean working shop office: concrete floor, a long steel-topped workbench running left to right across the lower third, plywood wall with a pegboard strip behind it, and a roll-up garage door on the right third of the back wall, fully open, showing a plain white work van with its rear doors open and no logos. Three people in plain dark work shirts: one man standing at the wall with a marker in front of a printed 12-month calendar covered in color blocks; one person at the middle of the bench at two monitors showing a simple pipeline board and a flow chart; one person at the right end at a video editing station with headphones and a timeline on screen. Two phones on stands, a tablet, a wall-mounted TV showing an agenda slide, printed ad proofs pinned in a grid on the pegboard, a rolling cart beside the bench. Neutral 5000K daylight plus overhead shop LEDs, bright and even, no dark overlay. No brand logos on any screen or object. Natural, unposed, real working moment. 4:5 aspect ratio.

Then edit down:
- **Level 2:** "Remove the person at the editing station and the editing monitors, remove the wall TV, the rolling cart and the ad proofs. Replace the 12-month calendar with a whiteboard reading 'RETARGET: visited, didn't book' and a small calendar with four blocks. Lower the roll-up door halfway. Keep the camera, bench, light and van exactly the same."
- **Level 1:** "Keep only one man seated at the left end of the bench with one open laptop, one phone face down, a notebook and a coffee mug. Remove everything else from the bench and wall except a small calendar with two dates circled. Close the roll-up door. Keep the camera, bench and light exactly the same."

### Reel timing

| Slide | Hold |
|---|---|
| 1, hook | 2.0 s |
| 2, Starter | 3.0 s |
| 3, Booked Growth | 3.0 s |
| 4, Market Dominator | 3.5 s |
| 5, CTA | 3.0 s |

Hard cuts on the beat, no zooms or pans: a locked frame that suddenly fills up is the whole effect. About 14.5 seconds, loops cleanly back to the hook. Use slide 1 as the Reel cover.
