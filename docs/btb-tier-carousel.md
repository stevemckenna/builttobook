## The visual system

One 4 x 4 grid of 16 tiles sits in the same position on every tier slide. Each tile is one thing the client gets, with an icon and a two-word label.

- **Included:** solid Concrete White tile, Jobsite Ink icon and label.
- **Not included:** dashed outline, dimmed icon and label still readable, so the viewer sees exactly what they are missing.
- **New at this level:** small Signal Green corner block with the check, plus a "New at this level" legend.
- **Counter:** big "5/16", "10/16", "16/16" beside the headline.
- **Level meter:** three blocks top right, filled 1, 2 or 3.

## Art direction

- **Ground:** Jobsite Ink `#151A18` with a subtle blueprint grid (`#212A26`, 40 px pitch) on every slide.
- **Top band:** Signal Green bar on the left edge, eyebrow in Arial Bold uppercase, headline in Arial Black uppercase with the final line in Signal Green, 6 px Blueprint Blue rule under it.
- **Bottom bar:** Signal Green, caption in Arial Bold, Jobsite Ink, sentence case.
- **Corners:** 4 px on tiles, 8 px on the button. No gradients, no shadows, no emoji, no Safety Orange.
- **Green discipline:** Signal Green only for the headline payoff, the "new" checks, the bottom bar and the CTA button.
- **Reel version:** same 4:5 slide centred in 9:16, with a plain Ink band top (BUILT TO BOOK in plain type) and bottom (left empty for the Reel interface). Swap the plain type for the reversed logo lockup when building in Canva.
