## Principle: lead with the pain (Confirmed direction)

Owners in pain often cannot see the solution, so open with the problem they feel, then show the way out. Solution-first copy ("automated follow-up") lands flat with someone who has not connected it to their pain. Every headline, post and ad should name a pain first.

**Headline rotation (Confirmed, Steve 2026-09-29):** No single lead pain is chosen. All of these are viable and must be tested and rotated: missed calls and slow follow-up; website looks worse than the work; not showing up on Google; owner tied to the phone 24/7. Track which pain performs best per platform and trade.

## 1. Ten pains and problems we solve (Draft)

1. Website looks worse than the work (embarrassment)
2. Missed calls while the crew is on a job (lost jobs, guilt)
3. Leads go cold because follow-up is slow
4. Quotes sent, then never chased
5. Hard to find on Google and Maps
6. Too few reviews
7. Past customers never re-engaged
8. Hard to hire; the company looks smaller or less serious online than it is
9. Owner tied to the phone 24/7 and doing admin by hand
10. Burned by past web guys; no idea what is working

## 2. Ten solutions on paper (Draft; not all will be used)

Free mockup; professionally built site; Google Business Profile setup; missed-call text-back; instant reply to every inquiry; follow-up sequence for non-responders; quote follow-up reminders; automatic review requests after each job; CRM pipeline and shared inbox; past-customer reactivation.

**New (Confirmed, Steve 2026-09-29):** Hiring support as an **add-on** to any package: careers page, job application form and a hiring pipeline in the CRM. Not yet scoped or priced; needs a spec and a price added to the package document before it is sold. Solves pain 8.

## 3. Fears (pain over a longer time horizon) (Draft)

A slow season with no pipeline; losing jobs to a competitor with weaker work but a better site; being unable to hire and burning out; letting customers down because they cannot answer every call; repeat and referral work quietly drying up.

## 4. Dreams and longer-horizon outcomes (Draft)

A booked-out schedule of good jobs; a team they are proud of; customers who rave; evenings and weekends back; a business that runs without the owner on the phone 24/7.

## 5. One-tenth versus ten-times exercise

Purpose: stretch thinking about what is possible at any price. Not all of it gets sold.

**Ten times (Confirmed ideas, Steve 2026-09-29): what we would add if price were no object.**
- On-site photo and video shoot of finished jobs and the crew (feeds the site and social)
- Done-for-you recruiting ads
- Monthly strategy session with the owner
- A voice receptionist that answers every call around the clock. Steve specified it would be AI, not a person. Client-facing wording should say what it does ("answers every call, books the job") and avoid the phrase "AI-powered" per the voice rules; be plain and honest that it is automated.

**One-tenth (Open):** what would we deliver at a fraction of today's price? Idea to test: a free or very low-cost audit or scorecard of the owner's Google and website. Steve chose to keep the four-package ladder, so treat this as a lead-in idea, not a new tier.

## 6. Non-negotiables in every offer (Draft)

Real Google profile; a site built from the owner's real work and approved before launch; fast reply to every inquiry; a review request after every job; monthly reporting.

## 7. Four core pillars, framed as steps (Draft)

1. **Look the part:** free mockup, website, Google profile
2. **Get found:** reviews, local pages, directories
3. **Answer fast:** missed-call text-back, follow-up, CRM, quote reminders
4. **Grow and keep:** past-customer reactivation, hiring support, monthly review

## 8. Four logistical pillars (Draft)

1. Free mockup and checkout
2. Kickoff and materials
3. Launch on the guaranteed date
4. Monthly management and reporting

## 9. Milestone map (Confirmed: keep the four packages)

The existing four-package ladder already works as the milestone map: each tier solves more of the ten pains. No new entry tier or premium tier for now (Steve 2026-09-29). Map tiers to pillars in the package copy when it is rebranded. Pricing and inclusions: package document only.
