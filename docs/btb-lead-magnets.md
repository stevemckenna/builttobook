# Built To Book: Lead Magnets

Last updated: 2026-09-29. Status: draft, in review. Nothing new has been built or published.

Source: a private training session on lead magnets. Private reference only: no quotes, no name-dropping.

Builds on: `docs/btb-offer-worksheet.md` (pillars), `docs/btb-conversion-ecosystem.md` (opt-in flow), `docs/btb-niche-profile.md` (pains).

Terms note: "lead magnet" is an internal word. To owners, call it a free mockup, guide or checklist.

---

## 1. Decision (Steve, 2026-09-29)

**The first lead magnet is the free website mockup, and all traffic is driven to it.** Every profile link, post, comment, email and DM points to the mockup request. No second magnet competes with it for now.

Destination today: the live page at https://try.built2book.live/home, whose main call to action is "Get a free website mockup" and which embeds the GHL request form. Use one tracked link per source (section 7).

## 2. Why the mockup works as the magnet

- It is a big, tangible result: the owner sees their own business on a real site before paying.
- It matches the pain "my website looks worse than my work", and shows the operator's eye for what a trades site needs.
- It is the sales mechanism itself: the mockup leads to a call and an offer.
- It is easy to understand in one line, which keeps every message simple.

Cost to watch: each mockup takes Steve's time. Protect it with a fit check (established business, real work to show, decision maker) before building, and a daily cap.

## 3. Adding a video audit (optional layer)

The conversion plan pairs the mockup with a short personal video audit of the owner's site and Google profile. Treat it as a bonus for owners who reply "got it", not as a second offer on the page. Keep the page and every message about one thing: the free mockup.

## 4. Self-serve guides are on hold

The earlier idea of a PDF-plus-video guide ("Show up on Google in 3 steps") is parked. Revisit it only if one of these becomes true:

- Mockup requests are more than Steve can build and traffic still needs somewhere to go.
- Data after about two weeks shows owners who are not ready for a mockup but would take a lighter first step.
- The mockup page converts poorly with cold traffic and a smaller first step would help.

If that happens, the pillar and level grid below is the starting point. The guide would then feed people toward the mockup, not compete with it.

## 5. Making the mockup offer stronger (page and message)

Work from the training's principle: one clear benefit, one action.

- **Headline test ideas** (rotate the lead pain, per the offer worksheet): "See your business on a website that matches your work, free." / "Your work is solid. See a website that shows it." / "Stop losing jobs to a bad first impression."
- **One field group only:** name, business, phone or email, website. Keep the form short.
- **What happens next, stated plainly** on the page and in the confirmation: how long the mockup takes, what Steve needs from them, no obligation, no card.
- **Proof, honestly:** real photos of real work when available; the operator credential; no invented reviews or numbers. Add proof to the page as soon as Steve has it.
- **Mobile first:** most owners will tap from Facebook or Instagram.

## 6. Video script (for a short explainer, optional)

If Steve films a short video explaining the mockup (for the page, the welcome email and pinned posts), keep it under 10 minutes and use this order:

| Step | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Confirmation | Who you are and what this covers | True credentials only: has run trade businesses, now builds sites and follow-up for them. No figures without Steve's approval |
| 2. The difference | Why this is not another agency pitch | Free mockup before paying; site plus follow-up as one system |
| 3. Story and bond | Owner as hero, using Steve's own story | Broad enough that owners see themselves; true moments only |
| 4. Payoff | Before, after, three steps | Steps: tell us about your work; we build your concept from your real jobs; you review it on a 15-minute call |
| 5. Transition | Recap and next step | Request the mockup; mention the packages only if asked |

Honesty rules: true stories only; AI imagery is atmosphere and labelled where it could look real; no results guarantee.

## 7. Where to send traffic, and how to track it

Put the mockup link in:

- Facebook page and Instagram bio
- Every daily video's caption or pinned comment, and under every third post
- Comment replies ("comment MOCKUP and I'll send the link")
- Pinned posts on each profile
- Welcome and offer emails
- YouTube Shorts and TikTok descriptions
- The Built To Book Google Business Profile, once created
- DMs to warm contacts
- Printed material and QR codes later, after trademark clearance

Use a different tracked link per source so results can be compared (bio, post, comment, email, DM, YouTube). Record in the KPI sheet: clicks, form starts, mockups requested, mockups sent, calls booked, sales. Judge each source by sales, not by requests alone.

## 8. Build order

1. Steve confirms the mockup as the only magnet, and the fit check and daily cap.
2. Test the live form end to end (contact created, notification fires).
3. Add tracked links per source and put them in bios and posts.
4. Steve finishes the pipeline so every request is tracked.
5. The assistant (with approval) can tighten the page copy and add a "what happens next" block.
6. After about two weeks, review whether a second magnet is needed.
