# Built To Book: Positioning

Source: positioning exercise from a private training video, worked through for Built To Book (2026-09-29). Private reference only. Do not quote it or name the source in any post.

Status key: **Confirmed** = Steve said it. **Draft** = Claude's assumption, Steve to correct.

Builds on: `btb-niche-profile.md`, `btb-offer-worksheet.md`, `btb-brand-of-you.md`. Reference this sheet when creating content. Review and update it every couple of months.

Rule: Do the comparison once, choose the ground, then stop watching competitors. They cost more attention than they take clients. Never name competitors in public content.

---

## 1. Competitive alternatives (what the ideal customer buys instead) (Confirmed)

1. DIY builders (Wix, Squarespace)
2. A local freelancer or a relative who does the site
3. A big home-service marketing agency

Not chosen: lead marketplaces (Angi, HomeAdvisor). Keep as a secondary comparison in content about owning your pipeline.

**Timing insight (Confirmed, Steve 2026-09-29):**
- Owners **starting their own company** are a good moment to engage: they need a presence and have no legacy.
- Owners with a **poor existing website** are offered a superior alternative.
Both are entry points for outreach and content, alongside established owners.

Specific named competitors at a similar level: none recorded. Steve to add if wanted (internal only).

## 2. Three distinct capabilities (Confirmed, exactly three)

1. **Free mockup before paying.** They see the work on their own business before they commit.
2. **Site plus follow-up as one system.** Website, CRM, missed-call text-back, follow-up and review requests together.
3. **Operator who has run trade businesses.** Steve has run and scaled a garage epoxy brand and a countertop fabricator, so he understands the details of running a trades company.

Supporting facts from the package document (not chosen as capabilities, but usable as proof points): launch date guarantee, month-to-month plans, client owns domain, Google profile and data. Verify wording against `docs/grnded-website-ghl-packages.md` before use.

## 4. Combine two things (Draft)

"The website and follow-up system built by someone who has run trades companies." Hard to copy because it pairs the presentation with the operating experience.

## 5. Best-fit client: niche inside the niche (Confirmed)

**Owners who lose leads while on the job.** This does not narrow the niche; it names who benefits most. It works for any trade where the phone rings while the crew is busy. It matches the lead pain rotation (missed calls and slow follow-up) and the enemy "chasing every lead by hand."

## 6. Market category (Confirmed)

**Marketing partner for trades.** Use these words in bios and content. Note: this is a broader category than "website" or "agency." Content should still make clear what the partner delivers (site, Google, follow-up, hiring support) so the category does not feel vague.
