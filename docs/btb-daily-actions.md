# Built To Book: Daily Actions

Last updated: 2026-09-29. Status: draft, in review. Numbers marked (proposed) are starting targets for Steve to confirm.

Source: a private training session on daily actions. Private reference only: no quotes, no name-dropping.

---

## 1. The idea

A short, fixed list of sales and content actions done every day, in about an hour, before anything else. Everything else (client work, builds, calls) comes after. Consistency matters more than volume: the same list every day beats a big push once a week.

Built To Book goal these actions serve: five new clients a month.

## 2. The daily list (about 60 minutes)

| # | Action | Target | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review yesterday's numbers (section 4) | 1 look, 3 numbers noted | 5 min |
| 2 | Check the GHL inbox and team channel; assign anything needed | Clear it | 5 min |
| 3 | Answer overnight DMs, comments and form leads (warm and hot first) | All answered | 10 min |
| 4 | Post today's short video (already filmed and scheduled) | 1 video | 5 min |
| 5 | Schedule tomorrow's video and any text post | Queued | 10 min |
| 6 | Plug the free mockup and video audit on yesterday's post (comment or story) | 1 plug | 2 min |
| 7 | Reach out to new people (proposed 10 a day; see section 3) | 10 | 10 min |
| 8 | Follow up with leads who have not replied or need a nudge | 10 (proposed 5 to start) | 8 min |
| 9 | Comment on posts by local trades owners and contractor groups | 20 (proposed 10 to start) | 10 min |
| 10 | Log numbers in the KPI sheet, then monitor and reply later in the day | Done | 5 min |

Total is slightly over an hour at full targets. Start at the reduced numbers and build up over two weeks.

Notes:

- **The daily email.** The training sends one every day. Built To Book's list is new, so we keep the decision from the conversion plan: a welcome sequence first, then two to three offer emails a week. Move to daily only if opens and replies stay healthy.
- **Order matters.** Do items 1 to 6 first. They are the fixed part. Reach-outs and comments flex if the day is short, but the video and DMs never skip.
- **Client check-ins** come after the list, along with sales calls, mockups and video audits. Those are the higher-leverage tasks the list clears space for.

## 3. What counts as a reach-out

The training reaches out cold to fifteen people a day. For Built To Book, split it into safe channels:

- **Warm DMs.** People who liked, commented on, shared or followed the page, and people who replied to a post. Ask a simple question, then send the opt-in link if they are interested. Starter scripts are in `docs/btb-mvf-map.md`.
- **Value-trade invitations.** Ask a trades owner for a 15-minute call to review how they handle missed calls and follow-up, then show the offer.
- **Local owner conversations.** Follow local owners and reply meaningfully to their posts so the first message is not cold.
- **Targeted cold email (later).** Only after the compliance check in `docs/btb-conversion-ecosystem.md` section 9. Not part of the daily count until then.

Guardrail: brand-new Facebook and Instagram accounts can be restricted for bulk cold DMs. Keep messages personal, one at a time, and stop if either platform shows a warning. No copy and paste blasts.

## 4. Daily KPIs

Track in one simple sheet (Google Sheets or the GHL dashboard). One row per day:

| KPI | Proposed daily target |
|---|---|
| Short videos posted | 1 |
| Text posts | 3 to 4 a week (not daily) |
| New people engaged (followed or connected) | 10 |
| Comments on other people's posts | 10 to 20 |
| New reach-outs | 10 to 15 |
| Follow-ups | 5 to 10 |
| Replies received | track only |
| Free mockups or audits requested | track only |
| Calls booked | track only |
| Sales | track only |

Weekly review: look at replies, mockups requested, calls booked and sales. Replace the illustrative rates in the MVF map with real ones after about two weeks. If a number is missed, ask why before changing the target. Not hitting a goal is fine; not knowing why is the problem.

## 5. Weekly rhythm

- **Daily:** the list above.
- **Once a week (about 30 minutes):** batch film the week's short videos and write the text posts, based on the October theme in `docs/btb-launch-and-content-plan.md`.
- **Twice a week:** record the personal video audits for people who replied "got it", within the daily cap.
- **Weekly:** send the two to three offer emails, and review the KPI sheet.

## 6. Build order

1. Steve confirms the proposed targets, or changes them.
2. Set up the KPI sheet (an assistant can create a Google Sheet on request, with a daily row and a weekly summary).
3. Steve builds the nine-stage pipeline so follow-ups and replies can be logged in one place.
4. Add a daily reminder (calendar block or a GHL task) so the hour is protected.
5. Run it for two weeks, then adjust.
