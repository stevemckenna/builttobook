## Why There Is a Monthly Plan

The website and follow-up system are active services. The monthly plan covers the technology and ongoing attention required to keep everything operating properly: hosting, security/SSL, forms and lead-delivery monitoring, CRM and automation access (where included), workflow checks and maintenance, reporting, package-specific updates, and client support. Required while the website, CRM, automations, or related services remain hosted and managed by Grnded Agency.

## Software and Messaging Usage

Subscription includes the first $10 of platform usage per month (phone calls, text messages, email verification, AI services). Usage above the included allowance is billed separately; unusual usage is monitored and flagged.

## What the Client Owns

- Their domain
- Their Google Business Profile
- Their contacts and customer data
- Their business information and supplied creative assets
- Their CompanyCam account and photos (the widget only displays them)

Grnded Agency retains its reusable templates, workflows, automation framework, and underlying systems.

If a client cancels, services remain active through the end of the paid billing period and contacts/business data can be exported. Hosted websites and automations stop when the subscription ends. A compatible GHL account transfer is available for a $297 transfer fee. Plans are billed month to month and require 30 days' cancellation notice.
